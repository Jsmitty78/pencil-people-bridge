# Security and Privacy

PENCIL Bridge is a fictional-data internship prototype. It is not approved for production employee records.

## Do not submit real employee data

Do not enter confidential, identifiable, health-related, employment, or legal information into a public deployment. A real pilot requires explicit company approval covering source access, departments, retention, anonymization, storage, HR permissions, and incident handling.

## Reporting a security or privacy concern

Please report potential vulnerabilities privately to the repository owner through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature when available. Do not include real employee records, credentials, access tokens, or confidential company messages in an issue.

Public issues are appropriate for non-sensitive bugs that can be reproduced with fictional data.

## Prototype boundaries

- Browser-local IndexedDB is used for demonstration state.
- Simulated connectors do not access live PENCIL systems.
- The optional Ollama integration is intended for local execution.
- Production authentication, authorization, encryption, audit logging, retention, and privacy review are not implemented.
- AI output is a review candidate and must never be treated as an employment or legal decision.
