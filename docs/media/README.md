## Story sources

- Jake’s supplied account identifies the ten-day build and the participants in the HR/engineering review photograph. The [project archive](../PROJECT_ARCHIVE.md) separately records the broader August 17–29 internship.
- The original `PENCIL_Bridge_Interview_Focused.pptx.pptx` supplies the 10-interview count, research themes, feedback areas, team lead roles, financial planning example and presentation outline. Its proposed integrations and impact figures are distinguished from the implemented prototype and measured outcomes.
- Jake’s LinkedIn post beginning “Two days into Fukuoka” supplies his early impressions: hands-on experience outside Kansai, PENCIL’s open office culture, engineer/consultant collaboration, the local community and capsule-hotel travel. The post was accessible in search-indexed activity on [Takeshi’s LinkedIn profile](https://jp.linkedin.com/in/takeshi-yamamoto-3a9339360); no direct post permalink was available. The story paraphrases those reflections rather than presenting newly invented memories.
- [README](../../README.md), [project archive](../PROJECT_ARCHIVE.md), [HR feedback](../hitomi-feedback.md), and [system-design alignment](../system-design-alignment.md) supply the documented product scope and implementation boundaries.

The supplied presentations and interview working files are not added to the public repository. This update adds photographs and narrative documentation; it does not modify application code or existing technical records.
