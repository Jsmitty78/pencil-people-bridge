# Hitomi-san Feedback

## Feedback summary

After reviewing three possible directions, Hitomi-san ranked them:

**3 → 2 → 1**

### Option 3
Support HR in objectively organizing issues between employees, managers, and HR.

Hitomi-san highlighted that facts and interpretations often become mixed together, and workplace concerns can be communicated emotionally. She felt AI could be valuable if it objectively organized the information before the human mediation process.

### Option 2
Support managers working with employees who have different needs and working styles.

Hitomi-san felt this could become especially powerful when combined with Option 3, so that after an issue is organized, managers can receive practical support on how to communicate with the individual.

## Direction chosen

The current prototype will combine **Option 3 + Option 2**.

### Part A: HR Issue Organizer
Separate:
- facts
- interpretations
- concerns / emotions
- missing information
- desired outcomes
- questions to clarify

### Part B: Manager Communication Support
Use only the employee's explicitly stated communication needs and working preferences to suggest a practical communication approach.

## Product principle

**AI organizes the problem. Humans handle the relationship.**

## Important boundary

The prototype must not diagnose, label, or infer employee conditions. It should support communication based on needs and preferences that the employee has actually communicated.
