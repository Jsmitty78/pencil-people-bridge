# Problem Statement

## Organizational challenge

As PENCIL grows and employs people with increasingly diverse backgrounds, needs, and working styles, HR and managers need to handle more situations where facts, interpretations, emotions, expectations, and support needs are mixed together.

This creates two related challenges:

1. HR spends significant human effort organizing employee concerns and mediating between employees and managers.
2. Managers may not always know how to adapt communication and support to each employee's stated needs and working preferences.

## Why this matters

The team initially explored translation and shared-understanding problems. After presenting three solution directions to HR, Hitomi-san ranked them:

**3 → 2 → 1**

She suggested that combining Option 3 and Option 2 could be the strongest approach.

## Validated solution direction

### Option 3: Organize workplace issues objectively

AI helps HR structure a concern into:

- Facts
- Interpretations
- Concerns / emotions
- Missing information
- Desired outcome
- Clarification questions
- Possible next steps

AI does not judge who is right or wrong.

### Option 2: Support managers working with different needs and working styles

After the situation is clarified, AI helps managers prepare communication based only on needs and preferences that the employee has explicitly stated.

Examples:

- prefers written follow-up
- needs priorities to be explicit
- prefers large tasks broken into smaller steps
- prefers time to process before responding
- prefers direct feedback

The system must never diagnose, label, or infer ADHD, autism, anxiety, or any other condition.

## Design principle

**AI organizes the problem. Humans handle the relationship.**
